# ZM Enterprises – Tailoring & Garment Management Web App

Welcome to **ZM Enterprises** Tailoring Management Suite! Designed specifically for bespoke tailoring, alterations, ethnic wear, uniforms, and garment solutions.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Dashboard & Production Overview**
   - Active orders tracking (Cutting, Stitching, Trial Ready, Completed, Delivered).
   - Upcoming trials & delivery alerts for the next 7 days.
   - Financial summaries: Total revenue, advance collected, pending balance due.

2. **Customer Records & Detailed Body Measurements**
   - Profile management for **Men, Women, and Children**.
   - Comprehensive tailor measurement chart (Upper body: Top Length, Chest/Bust, Upper Waist, Shoulder, Sleeve Length, Armhole, Neck, Front/Back Neck Depth; Lower body: Length, Hip, Waist, Thigh, Knee, Bottom Opening/Mori, Inseam).
   - Printable individual measurement sheets for workshop tailors.

3. **Order Lifecycle & Tracking**
   - Multi-category support: **Custom Stitching**, **Ethnic Wear** (Sherwanis, Lehengas, Anarkalis), **Alterations**, **Uniforms** (School/Corporate batches), and **Ready-to-Wear**.
   - Fabric source tracking (Customer Provided vs. ZM Sourced).
   - Trial fitting dates and final delivery deadlines.
   - Real-time status selector (`Received` → `Cutting` → `Stitching` → `Trial Ready` → `Completed` → `Delivered`).

4. **Tax Invoices & Customer Receipts**
   - Professional, branded invoices with ZM Enterprises letterhead.
   - Breakdown of item description, tailoring costs, advance paid, and balance due.
   - 1-click **Print / Save as PDF** formatted specifically for standard A4 and receipt printers.

5. **Data Protection & Offline Reliability**
   - Operates completely offline directly in any modern browser.
   - Automatically saves all updates in your browser's `localStorage`.
   - **One-click JSON backup export and import** to prevent data loss.
