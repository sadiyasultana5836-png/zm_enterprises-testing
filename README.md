# ZM Enterprises – Real Tailoring Business Management System

Welcome to the **ZM Enterprises** Tailoring Business Management Suite! A complete production, client measurement, and invoicing management system built specifically for professional tailor shops and ateliers.

---

## 🌟 Comprehensive Features

### 1. 👥 Customer Registration & Detailed Measurements
- Profiles for **Men, Women, and Kids**.
- Standard tailor measurement chart:
  - **Upper Body:** Top Length, Chest/Bust, Upper Waist, Shoulder, Sleeve Length, Armhole/Bicep, Neck/Collar, Front Neck Depth, Back Neck Depth.
  - **Lower Body:** Bottom Length, Hip, Lower Waist, Thigh, Knee, Bottom Mori, Inseam.
  - Fit preferences & special workshop notes (lining, shoulder pads, concealed zips).
- Instant customer search by **Name**, **Phone Number**, or **Locality**.
- **Customer Profile & History Modal:** Displays full body measurements chart + historical orders, amount spent, and direct WhatsApp contact.
- One-click **Printable Measurement Sheet** for workshop tailors.

### 2. ✂️ Order Creation & Production Lifecycle Tracking
- Multi-category support: **Custom Stitching**, **Ethnic Wear** (Sherwanis, Anarkalis, Lehengas), **Alterations**, **Uniforms** (School & Corporate batches), and **Ready-to-Wear**.
- Fabric source tracking (*Customer Provided* vs *ZM Sourced*).
- Priority flag: **Standard** vs **Express / Urgent**.
- Real-time production stages:
  `Received` ➔ `Cutting` ➔ `Stitching` ➔ `Trial Ready` ➔ `Completed` ➔ `Delivered`.

### 3. 📅 Trial Date & Delivery Date Management
- Dedicated **Trial Fitting Date** and **Final Delivery Date** for every order.
- Intelligent timeline alerts:
  - 👗 **Trials Scheduled Today / Next 3 Days** banner on Dashboard.
  - 📦 **Deliveries Due in 7 Days** counter.
  - ⚠️ **Overdue Deliveries Alert** with 1-click filter for delayed orders.

### 4. 📱 One-Click WhatsApp Notification Buttons
Direct integration with WhatsApp Web & App (`https://wa.me/...`) with 5 tailor-made notification templates:
1. **Order Booking Confirmation:** Order #, items, trial & delivery dates, advance received, and balance due.
2. **Trial Fitting Ready Alert:** Informs the customer that their outfit is ready for trial fitting at the studio.
3. **Order Completed & Pickup Ready:** Alerts customer that tailoring is finished and ready for pickup with outstanding balance reminder.
4. **Delivered & Thank You:** Post-delivery thank you message with fit satisfaction guarantee note.
5. **Payment Receipt & Balance Reminder:** Itemized payment breakdown and balance reminder.

### 5. 🏷️ Tailoring Service Catalog & Rate Card
- Interactive service catalog covering Bespoke Suits, Sherwanis, Bridal Lehengas, Anarkalis, Padded Blouses, Salwar Kameez, Uniform Sets, and Alterations.
- Displays standard starting prices, turnaround days, and fabric recommendations.
- **"+ Book This Service"** button instantly launches the Order Creation modal pre-filled with the service title and price!

### 6. 💳 Payment Tracking & Transaction Logs
- Initial advance collection and live balance calculation.
- **"Record Payment"** modal allows logging multiple installment payments (Cash, UPI/GPay/PhonePe, POS Card, Bank Transfer) with date and transaction notes.
- Automatically marks orders as **Fully Paid** once balance reaches zero.

### 7. 🧾 Branded Invoices & Printable Receipts
- Clean, professional Tax Invoice with **ZM Enterprises** header, contact info, customer bill-to, garment specs, payment breakdown, and terms.
- **Share via WhatsApp** button directly sends the invoice summary to the customer.
- **Print / Save as PDF** layout designed for standard A4 paper and 80mm receipt printers.

### 8. 🎨 Professional Green & Black Aesthetic
- Luxury atelier palette: Deep Black (`#070a10`), Rich Emerald Green (`#059669`, `#10b981`), and Warm Gold accents (`#d97706`).
- **Fully Mobile Responsive:** Sliding navigation drawer, touch-friendly buttons, and responsive grid layouts.
- Complete offline reliability with `localStorage` persistence and **Export / Import JSON Data Backup**.
