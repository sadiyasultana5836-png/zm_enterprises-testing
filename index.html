Industrial Atelier & Production Infrastructure

Garment Manufacturing & Fabric Trading Capacity

ZM Enterprises operates a dedicated tailoring workshop in Hyderabad equipped for both high-capacity batch manufacturing and bespoke precision. We maintain direct relationships with textile mills to guarantee certified uniform fabrics, unyielding stitch integrity, and transparent wholesale pricing.

Our 6-Stage Manufacturing Workflow

01

Pattern Grading & CAD Drafting

Standardized age-wise grading for school uniforms and personalized posture-corrected patterns for bespoke clients.

02

Precision Multi-Layer Cutting

Automated and high-precision knife cutting ensuring zero grain distortion across 50 to 500 layers of fabric simultaneously.

03

Industrial Batch Stitching

Reinforced double seams, 12-14 stitches per inch (SPI), high-tensile poly-core thread, and bar-tacking on stress corners.

04

High-Density Crest Embroidery

Multi-head computerized embroidery machines applying institutional crests, monograms, and badges with color-fast thread.

05

3-Point Quality Inspection

Every piece is checked for measurement adherence, seam tension, button attachment pull-strength, and loose thread trimming.

06

Department-Wise Tagged Dispatch

Ironing with industrial vacuum steam tables, individual poly-wrapping, and sorted packaging by class, house, or company department.
